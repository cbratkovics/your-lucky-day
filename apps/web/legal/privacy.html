Privacy

Effective September 30, 2026

Short version: we don't know who you are, and we'd like to keep it that way.

What the site stores

  • A random id generated in your browser, so your daily call is counted once. It isn't linked to you.
  • Your call history, streak and sparkle, stored only in your browser (localStorage).
  • If you buy the charm pack: the Polar checkout id, so it can't be reused, and an unlock token in your browser. Your name, email and payment details stay with Polar and its payment processor; we never receive card numbers.

What we don't do

No accounts. No ad networks. No analytics beyond aggregate tallies (how many people called each charm). No IP addresses stored. No selling or sharing of data. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, whose infrastructure logs may briefly process your IP to serve the page.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but purchases are for adults or with a parent's permission.

Your choices

Clear your browser's site data to erase everything we hold about your device. To ask about a purchase record, email the address below with your Polar receipt.
